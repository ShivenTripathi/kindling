🔥 Kindling

Kindling turns tabs you left open into a clean EPUB and emails it to your Kindle. Nothing leaves your machine except that one email.

Find it in Amazon's Content & Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings. Must end in kindle.com.

Sender

This only grants permission to send mail as you — Kindling can never read your inbox.
